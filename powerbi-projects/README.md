# 📊 Power BI Business Intelligence & Analytics Suite

A comprehensive collection of production-grade **Microsoft Power BI (.pbix)** interactive dashboards and business intelligence models developed for enterprise analytics, e-commerce telemetry, automotive diagnostics, and global supply chain intelligence.

Developed with **Power BI Desktop**, **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**, **Power Query ETL**, and interactive data visualization principles (*Techpile Technology Data Analytics*).

---

## 🌟 Featured Dashboards

### 1. 🛍️ Flipkart E-Commerce Sales & Customer Intelligence (`flipkart final project.pbix`)
- **Executive KPIs**: Total Sales Turnover, Average Order Value (AOV), Average Customer Rating, Total Quantity Sold.
- **Geographic Sales Analysis**: Regional revenue distribution (North, South, East, West) with geospatial mapping.
- **Category Performance**: Top product verticals (Electronics, Fashion & Apparel, Home & Kitchen, Beauty, Sports & Fitness).
- **Payment Gateway Split**: Channel breakdown across UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Sales performance correlated against star ratings (1★ to 5★).
- **Interactive Slicers**: Dynamic filtering by Payment Method and Category.

---

### 2. 🚗 Tesla & Automotive Safety Telemetry Dashboard (`Car interactive dashboard.pbix`)
- **Fleet Diagnostics**: Average Odometer mileage, Pricing distribution, EMI financing models across Tesla models (Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y).
- **Safety & Incident Telemetry**: Percentage of vehicles with accident history vs. clean title (% Without Accident vs. % With Accident).
- **Driver Assistance Systems (DAS)**: Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) pricing vs. safety correlation.
- **Geographic Density**: State-wise vehicle pricing and mileage analysis across USA states.

---

### 3. 🌍 Global Superstore & Supply Chain Analytics (`global _final_project.pbix`)
- **Worldwide Revenue Mapping**: Sales and profit distribution across 140+ countries.
- **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth**: Monthly and quarterly multi-year sales performance area trajectories.
- **Customer Segmentation**: Sales and profitability funnel across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- **Repeat Order Rate**: Customer lifetime value (LTV) and churn tracking.
- **Discount & Margin Telemetry**: Discount sensitivity analysis preventing margin erosion.

---

### 4. 🏥 Human Health & Clinical Risk Intelligence (`Human Health powerbi.pbix`)
- **Clinical Demographics**: Patient population distribution by age groups, gender, and regional cohorts.
- **Lifestyle Risk Factors**: Smoking status and cigarettes per day correlated with long-term clinical outcomes.
- **Cardiovascular Health**: Blood Pressure (BP Risk) classification and multi-variable health index.
- **Organ Health Correlation**: Organ condition telemetry and risk progression tracking.

---

## 💻 How to Open & Inspect Locally

1. Install **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** (Free download from [powerbi.microsoft.com](https://powerbi.microsoft.com)).
2. Double-click any of the `.pbix` files in this directory:
   - `flipkart final project.pbix`
   - `Car interactive dashboard.pbix`
   - `global _final_project.pbix`
   - `Human Health powerbi.pbix`
3. Power BI Desktop will launch with all pre-calculated DAX measures, data relationships, and interactive filters ready for exploration.
